# Withdrawn 3B locality/globality/K-complex follow-up

> **WITHDRAWN — no current numerical or mechanistic conclusion.** The exploratory scripts are
> hard-stopped, their whole-stage-shift null does not preserve local HR trends or clustered SO
> timing, and their old outputs are not part of the v9 production artifact contract.

The former document interpreted exploratory subgroup differences as evidence about SO globality,
autonomic-region localization, and K-complex mechanisms. Those interpretations are unsupported:

- Naji et al. detected SOs from F3 and F4 during R&K Stage 2 and SWS. It did not classify the
  detected Stage 2 events as K-complexes or test whether K-complexes carried the reported
  SO–HR relationship. An isolated frontal trough-time rule is not a morphology-validated
  K-complex detector.
- A fraction-of-contacts consensus score depends on clinical electrode count, placement,
  reference, and detectability. It is not a validated measure of scalp SO “globality.”
- Destrieux frontal/cingulate/insula groupings do not establish sampling of an autonomic network,
  and clinical coverage cannot support a negative regional-localization conclusion.
- Comparing exploratory iEEG subgroup magnitudes with Naji’s scalp Stage 2/SWS values cannot
  identify modality, spatial averaging, anatomy, reference, staging, or event type as the cause of
  a difference.

Globality, anatomical localization, and K-complex specificity remain **untested hypotheses**. A
future study would need preregistered definitions, morphology-validated events, expert sleep
staging, suitable scalp/iEEG reference and polarity controls, anatomically planned coverage, and a
local-trend/dependence-preserving event-locking null. Until then, do not use the removed historical
numbers to claim that global waves recover coupling, that a lobe does or does not carry coupling,
that K-complexes explain Naji’s Stage 2 result, or that lateral iEEG explains a scalp/iEEG
difference.
