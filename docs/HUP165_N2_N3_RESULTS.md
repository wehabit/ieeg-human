## What this subject shows

**3D — SO→spindle coupling is ~7.6× stronger in N2-like than N3-like sleep**
(MI 1.35×10⁻⁴ vs 1.78×10⁻⁵). Direction matches the expectation that spindle-related coupling is a
lighter-NREM phenomenon. **But both values are negligible in absolute terms** — Tort MI is
normalised 0–1, so 10⁻⁴ is essentially no coupling. The impressive-looking MI_z of 28.8 is inflated
by the sample count shrinking the surrogate SD, not by a large effect. Across the cohort this
N2 > N3 direction did **not** reach significance (n = 17, p = 0.40).

**3B — SO→heartbeat coupling is present and essentially equal in both stages**
(z = 4.01 vs 4.41; modulation ≈ 0.53 bpm either way). Heart rate does shift systematically around the
slow-oscillation trough in this subject, and it does so no differently in light vs deep NREM. Pooled
across the whole night it is stronger still (z = 7.20) simply because more events enter the average.

**3A — could not be computed per stage.** N2-like and N3-like alternate on a minutes timescale, and
the longest contiguous N2-like block was only **10 min** — far short of what a 0.02 Hz (50 s)
coherence estimate needs. Only the pooled-NREM block (58 min) was long enough.

**3A pooled — this subject is one of the 7/23 that cleared threshold** (0.180 vs 0.139). That looked
like evidence for the LC infraslow rhythm. It is not: the cohort-wide frequency-specificity control
showed an **11.6% background exceedance at arbitrary frequencies**, with 0.02 Hz ranking only 2nd of
63 bins. This subject's 0.180 sits inside that broadband elevation rather than on a ~50 s peak. See
[LC_INFRASLOW_3ABD_SUMMARY.md](LC_INFRASLOW_3ABD_SUMMARY.md) §4.

## Caveats specific to this subject

- Median heart rate **91.8 bpm** during NREM — tachycardic for sleep. Anti-seizure medication and/or
  epilepsy may blunt autonomic modulation, which would work against 3A and 3B.
- Sigma power shows transients reaching **2500% of median**. These are **not** epileptiform —
  IED masking (20–80 Hz detection) reduced the top-50 power bins by a median of only 0.4%. They are
  large sigma-band events (spindles/arousals), a property of the data rather than contamination.
- Slow oscillations were detected on the **channel average**, which attenuates them where contacts
  are not synchronous. This likely suppresses both 3B and 3D and should be redone per channel.
