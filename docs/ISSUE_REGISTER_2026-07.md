## What SWA means

**SWA** is **slow-wave activity**: broadband EEG/iEEG power at approximately **0.5–4 Hz** in the
human Lecci analysis. It tends to increase with deeper NREM. SWA is not an individual slow
oscillation (SO), and neither SWA nor an SO is an LC measurement.

## C. Paper-method evidence

### Lecci et al. 2017

[Primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5298853/):

- human scalp EEG/ECG, with SWA 0.5–4 Hz and sigma 10–15 Hz;
- artifact/movement-free visually scored NREM from the first 210 min after sleep onset;
- four-cycle Morlet power every 0.1 s and mandatory symmetric 4 s smoothing;
- second Morlet analysis over 0.001–0.12 Hz, bouts ≥120 s, duration-weighted, with
  approximately 0.02-Hz modulation;
- three-Gaussian fit, fitted spectral SD, and peak-window mean;
- 120 s z-scored sigma/HR intervals, HR as source wave, averaged within then across participants;
- FSP visually identified from overlapping artifact-free NREM spectra;
- LC/brainstem mechanisms discussed as possibilities, not measured in humans.

### Naji et al. 2019

[Primary manuscript](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5393b9zk):

- scalp PSG F3/A2 and F4/A1 derivations in uninterrupted 3-min bins;
- ECG 0.5–100 Hz, Pan–Tompkins R-wave detection with visual confirmation;
- RR resampled at 4 Hz with a piecewise cubic spline;
- SOs at 0.15–4 Hz with Dang-Vu morphology criteria;
- one 10 s SO-triggered RR/HR average per derivation;
- HR maximum/RR minimum time per derivation, then average derivation times;
- principal endpoint: visit-specific SO–HR timing related to perceptual speed, not an LC measure.

### Staresina et al. 2015 and Helfrich et al. 2018

[Staresina full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4625581/) and
[Helfrich full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5754239/):

- SO 0.16–1.25 Hz and fixed spindle 12–16 Hz;
- complete SO cycle/amplitude and spindle RMS/duration criteria;
- artifact-free NREM and manual/visual validation;
- Helfrich takes one maximal spindle amplitude and corresponding SO phase per SO epoch;
- neither paper validates LC activity.

### Osorio-Forero et al. 2021

[Final article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2021.09.041) and
[primary preprint/methods](https://doi.org/10.1101/2021.03.08.434399):

- direct LC/terminal optogenetics, thalamic NE measurement, receptor blockade, and autonomic
  pharmacology establish a noradrenergic mechanism in mice;
- they do not validate this branch’s human iEEG/ECG measures as LC-specific.

### Jacobsen et al. 2026

[eLife reviewed preprint](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/110252):

- mouse experiments combine cortical NE measurements with LC optogenetics and heart-rate
  recording, adding causal support for LC/NE contributions to infraslow cardiac dynamics;
- the human reanalysis measures HR, sigma power, and memory, not LC neurons or NE;
- the eLife review notes that some human associations were not sigma-specific; and
- it strengthens the biological rationale for a candidate proxy but does not validate the HUP
  scalp/iEEG/ECG endpoints as LC-specific.

### Demuru et al. 2022 / RESPect ds003848

[Dataset article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12021-022-09567-6) and
[OpenNeuro dataset](https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds003848.v1.0.3):

- the public BIDS release includes subject/run `events.tsv` and subject `electrodes.tsv` sidecars,
  not only raw signals and `channels.tsv`;
- the pinned annotation snapshot (`93f494138dbf4c24f9b84323e994193aa26b3386`) contains coarse
  sleep, transition, artifact, seizure, and curated SWS/REM-selection intervals used in A63;
- the electrode sidecars contain atlas, SOZ, resection, edge, tissue, and pathology fields used
  conservatively in A64; and
- these metadata support exclusions and coarse state constraints, not AASM/R&K N2/N3 validation or
  direct LC measurement.

## D. Publication acceptance gate

Do not promote descriptive observations to confirmatory, replication, or LC-specific claims until
all of the following pass:

1. every expected participant is completed or has one prespecified, documented exclusion;
2. every cache/result has the current schema, cache-producer digest, complete configuration,
   source/code/runtime provenance, and successful terminal manifest;
3. no acquisition failures; endpoint-local support rules justified independently of result
   direction and accompanied by the locked coverage/window/event sensitivity grids;
4. expert/validated staging and anatomy/SOZ/bad-contact QC;
5. blinded missing-boundary/ECG/SO/spindle/artifact detector validation;
6. 3A reference-implementation benchmark;
7. no 3D p value until a pairing-aware null repeats full event pairing and contact aggregation;
   do not report N2-like/N3-like until its additional matched-contact/count-controlled test is
   validated;
8. synthetic tests plus real-cache coherence calibration;
9. corrected summaries generated from only current outputs, with explicit endpoint status and
   denominators;
10. confidence intervals and equivalence logic appropriate to any absence claim.

Until then, the accurate conclusion is:

> The repository now contains safer, tested estimators for LC-motivated downstream physiology.
> Endpoint-local v9 QC recovers subject-level estimates, most consistently a small SO-linked
> cardiac acceleration, while 3A is heterogeneous and 3D remains descriptive. Profile sensitivity,
> absent construct validation, and disabled 3B/3D inference prevent proof of human LC tracking.
